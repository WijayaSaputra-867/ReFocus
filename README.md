# Refocus

> Take back your attention.

Refocus is an app designed to help people regain focus by making it harder to spend too much time on distracting apps. Instead of simply tracking time one app at a time, it looks at the apps you choose and monitors how long you use them overall. Once the limit is reached, the app gives you a pause so you can stop, reset, and return to what matters.

## Why this app exists

Many people want to be more focused, but it is easy to lose track of time when using social media, videos, games, or other distracting apps. Refocus is built for that problem.

It helps by:

- letting you choose the apps you want to protect
- tracking total distraction time across those apps
- reminding you when you have reached your limit
- giving you a break before you can continue
- helping you build healthier digital habits over time

## How it works

Imagine you choose TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube as apps you want to limit. If you spend too much time on them in a single day, Refocus starts counting the total time spent across those apps.

Once the total reaches the limit you set, the app enters a cooldown period. During this time, access to the protected apps is paused or restricted so you have a chance to step away and regain control.

This makes it easier to manage distraction without needing to check each app separately. Refocus treats the selected apps as one focus system.

## Main features

- Select apps you want to protect
- Track combined distraction time across multiple apps
- Set a limit before the break starts
- Set how many focus sessions you allow per day
- Set a cooldown period after a distraction session
- Limit access to distracting apps when the threshold is reached
- Save settings locally on the device
- No account required for the basic experience

## Simple example

For example:

- Trigger time: 5 minutes
- Daily limit: 5 sessions
- Cooldown: 15 minutes

If you spend enough time in protected apps to reach the trigger, Refocus starts the first session. After that, the protected apps go into a cooldown state for 15 minutes. Once the cooldown ends, the cycle can begin again the next day or after a reset.

## Who is it for?

Refocus is useful for:

- people who easily lose time on social media
- students who need help staying focused while studying
- workers who want less distraction during the day
- gamers or video watchers who want healthier screen habits
- anyone trying to use their phone in a more intentional way

## Privacy and security

Refocus is designed with privacy in mind. It keeps the important data on the device instead of sending it to a server. The goal is to give users more control and less pressure while still creating a helpful experience.

## How to use it

1. Open the app.
2. Choose the apps you want to control.
3. Set the time limit and cooldown.
4. Turn protection on.
5. Use the app normally and let Refocus help you stay more focused.

## Project status

Refocus is still in an early development stage, but the core idea and main user experience are already in place. The project continues to evolve as real usage and Android platform constraints are better understood.
